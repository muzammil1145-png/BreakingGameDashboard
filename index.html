Traffic Findings

Organic Search provides a strong combination of volume and conversion. It generated 79,556 pageviews and 378 checkout sessions.

Email and Community/Referral show strong reported conversion efficiency at low volume. These channels are candidates for controlled expansion testing.

Paid Social generates substantial traffic but weak downstream conversion. Meta generated 91,336 pageviews while recording the lowest reported channel conversion rate.

Checkout Findings

Several high-interest products show substantial checkout-to-order gaps. Secret Hitler, We're Doomed!, MIXTAPE, Sparkle*Kitty and several Dwellings products warrant further checkout-friction analysis.

Legendary Upgrade has the strongest observed completion among products with matched orders. Approximately 25% of combined observed activity is represented by completed orders, followed by Oracle Hex Tile at 23.6% and We're Doomed! at 19%.

MIXTAPE shows a particularly large observed checkout-to-order gap. Only 5.9% of its combined observed activity is represented by matched orders.

International abandoned checkouts are associated with materially higher shipping costs. Average shipping is $8.39 for the U.S., compared with $23.66 for Canada, $30.83 for the United Kingdom, and $35.20 for Germany.

Q4 Recommendations

Priority actions based on revenue, traffic, advertising, and checkout performance.

  1. Protect the products driving the majority of revenue. Prioritize availability and replenishment for the approximately 13 products responsible for 80% of revenue.
  2. Prioritize Organic Search and run controlled Email and Community/Referral growth tests. Organic provides the strongest combination of scale and reported conversion efficiency, while Email and Referral show promising efficiency at smaller volumes.
  3. Reallocate Meta attention toward campaigns with clearer efficiency signals. Maintain Traffic TOF, test Dwellings LPV and Sales ATC, and audit the $12.9K DA | Sales campaign before additional spending.
  4. Investigate checkout friction for high-gap products and international customers. Review product pricing, availability, shipping transparency, and international shipping costs before Q4.
  5. Improve attribution infrastructure. Capture campaign and session identifiers through checkout so future reporting can connect Meta spend directly to orders, revenue, CPA, and ROAS.
